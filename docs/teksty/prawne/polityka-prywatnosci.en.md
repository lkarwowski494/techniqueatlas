## In short

The Technique Atlas website itself does not collect your personal data. It has no accounts, forms or ads. It uses no cookies, no browser storage, no analytics and no tracking tools. <!-- D-1, D-2, D-3, D-4 -->
The site loads only its own files. Search and filters run in your browser. <!-- D-5, D-6 -->
The site is served by a hosting service that logs your IP address on every visit for security purposes (details below). <!-- D-8 -->
Questions: {{kontakt}}.

## Who runs the site

The site is run by {{administrator}}, contact: {{kontakt}}.

## What happens when you open the site

- Your browser downloads the site's files (HTML, CSS, scripts, exercise drawings) from the hosting server. The site loads nothing from other services: no fonts, scripts, images or plugins. <!-- D-5 -->
- The hosting server logs your IP address on every visit and stores it for security purposes. These logs are kept by the hosting provider, a company based in the United States, under its own terms. Data may therefore be processed in the United States. The provider states that it complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (a European Commission adequacy decision). <!-- D-8, D-10; [DO KONSULTACJI] PP-3 -->
- The site has no visitor statistics of its own. <!-- D-9 → D-4 (06-tresci-004) -->

## Search, filters and drawings

- Text you type into the exercise name search is not sent anywhere. Your browser downloads one file from the site with the list of names in that language and searches it on your device. <!-- D-6 -->
- List filters run in your browser and do not change the page address. <!-- D-6 -->
- The animated exercise drawing loads the site's own data file. If "Reduce motion" is turned on in your system settings, the animation does not start by itself. Only your browser reads this setting. <!-- D-7 -->
- The site also works without JavaScript: the exercise list and descriptions are plain HTML. <!-- D-11 -->

## Links to other services

- The "App Store" button leads to the app's page on the App Store. The address carries the campaign tag `techniqueatlas-<language>`. It only says which language version of the site the visit came from, not who you are. After you follow the link, the app store's terms apply. <!-- D-12 -->
- On iPhone and iPad, the browser may show an app banner. The browser shows it itself, based on a tag in the page code. <!-- D-13 [DO USTALENIA] T-2 -->
- The language switcher and all other links lead to pages of this site.

## When you write to us

The site has no contact form. <!-- D-3 --> If you email {{kontakt}}, we handle your message as described in the "When you write to us" section of the app's privacy policy: {{politykaAplikacji}}. That section covers purpose, legal basis, retention, recipients and your rights.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to object and to data portability. You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).
Because the site itself collects no personal data, we hold no data we could provide or delete, except emails you send us. For the hosting logs, write to us and we will tell you who the provider is and how to contact them.

## Do you have to provide data

No. You do not provide any data to use the site.

## Automated decisions

We make no decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling.

## Children

The site itself collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If what the site does with data changes, we will update this policy before publishing that version and give a new date. The site's code is public, so every change is visible in its repository history. <!-- D-14 -->
